The original counts of pageviews are not included in the dataset for privacy reasons.
Instead, the original counts are replaced by privacy-protected counts (i.e., ```dp_views```).
Specifically, each original count is replaced with a randomly drawn value from a normal distribution centered around the original, unprotected count.
