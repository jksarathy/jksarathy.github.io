The original counts of pageviews are not included in the dataset for privacy reasons.
Instead, the original counts are replaced by privacy-protected counts (i.e., ```rounded_views```).
Specifically, each original count is replaced by that count rounded up to the next 100 (e.g., a count of 1,001 would be reported as 1,100).
Figure 2 depicts this process.

Records with a ```rounded_views ``` value less than or equal to 1,000 were not included in the dataset to further protect privacy.
